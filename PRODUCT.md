# Inside the Runtime

## Register

brand

## Users

People reviewing Srijit Gyawali's engineering work, projects and contact information on desktop and mobile.

## Product Purpose

Present backend, distributed systems and Web3 work through an immersive portfolio with useful project details and direct contact links.

## Brand Personality

Precise, kinetic, technical. Preserve the existing dark scene, acid-green signals, condensed display typography and procedural WebGL world.

## Anti-references

Tiny technical labels, low-contrast text, generic portfolio cards, animation that makes content harder to read, and invented project claims.

## Design Principles

- Visitors can read content without zooming, waiting for a reveal or pausing animation.
- Supporting details deserve clear hierarchy as much as large headlines.
- Motion expresses the underlying systems and remains reversible.
- Project structure and genuine engineering details supply the character.
- Content and navigation work independently of WebGL.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

High-contrast body text, readable mobile type, keyboard navigation, generous touch targets, native dialogs, reduced motion and a manual motion control. These preserve the existing accessibility intent and address the owner's specific readability feedback.
